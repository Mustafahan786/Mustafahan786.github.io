Research profile

Engineering high-performance physical systems.

My research spans feedback control, precision mechatronics, embedded systems, robotics, and machine learning. I am interested in the complete engineering chain: understanding system dynamics, designing control algorithms, implementing them on hardware, and validating performance experimentally.

My current work focuses on increasing the closed-loop bandwidth and imaging speed of contact-mode Atomic Force Microscopy through system identification, loop shaping, embedded digital control, and resonance-aware filtering.
