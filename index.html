About

Engineering intelligent systems that interact with the physical world.

My research interests span feedback control, precision mechatronics, embedded systems, robotics, and machine learning. I am especially interested in improving the performance of physical systems by combining accurate dynamic models, real-time control, sensing, and computational intelligence.

My current work focuses on increasing the closed-loop bandwidth and imaging speed of contact-mode Atomic Force Microscopy through system identification, loop shaping, embedded digital control, and resonance-aware filtering.

Current research directions

Work centered on high-performance physical systems, from plant dynamics to real-time embedded implementation.

01

Primary research

High-Speed Atomic Force Microscopy

Closed-loop Z-axis control for contact-mode AFM, with emphasis on bandwidth extension, scan-speed improvement, resonance suppression, and quantitative imaging performance.
