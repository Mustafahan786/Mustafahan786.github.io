About

Engineering intelligent systems that interact with the physical world.

My work focuses on feedback control, precision engineering, embedded implementation, robotics, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in high-performance control for mechatronic systems and research that connects physical system dynamics with modern intelligent algorithms.

Research

Current research directions

High-Speed Atomic Force Microscopy

Closed-loop Z-axis control, system identification, resonance suppression, loop shaping, and scan-speed improvement for contact-mode AFM.

Embedded Feedback Control

Real-time digital control on STM32 platforms, high-rate ADC/DMA acquisition, PI control, digital filters, and hardware-aware implementation.

Precision Mechatronics

Model-based analysis and control of piezoelectric actuators, sensing electronics, amplifiers, and precision motion systems.

Robotics & Physical AI

Robotics, reinforcement learning, rehabilitation systems, and intelligent control for machines that operate in the physical world.
